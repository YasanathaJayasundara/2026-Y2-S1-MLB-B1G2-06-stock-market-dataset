FINAL MODEL-COMPARISON SUMMARY

Problem: Predict next closing-price direction (0 = down/not up, 1 = up)
Models compared: Logistic Regression, SVM, Decision Tree, KNN, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting
Majority-class accuracy baseline: 0.5456
Primary selected model: KNN
Secondary comparison model: Decision Tree

Finding:
KNN was the only model that predicted both classes in substantial quantities and achieved
the highest test ROC-AUC and balanced accuracy. Overall predictive discrimination remained
weak because every model's ROC-AUC was close to 0.50.
